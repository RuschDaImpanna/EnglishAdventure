What's English Adventure!?

It is a learning initiative for the educational institution IED Rincón Santo in Cajicá - Cundinamarca - Colombia.



This project was designed as an innovating english learning stategy for high school students with content as readings, videos, music and videogames; all of this, also on a platform that shows the progress and grades of the course.‎
