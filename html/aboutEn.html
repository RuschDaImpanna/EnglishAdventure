What's English Adventure!?

This project created by Viviana Andrea Castañeda, who holds a Master’s degree in Neuropsychology and Education, aims to provide a playful and educational resource to be used in secodary school English classrooms.



This initiative has been developed based on MEd. Viviana's classroom experience.

Thanks to her knowledge, English Adventure! has been able to establish at IED Rincón Santo, located in the municipality of Cajicá, Cundinamarca in Colombia

What does this platform offers?

This platform is a tool that connects students with material for studying English.‎



All lections, works and all resources are on a hub that blends art and English-speaking culture with grammar concepts, vocabulary and, most important, the active practice of the language.‎

It's not just good for students.

The teacher also can interact with the platform: adding and creating material desired, and having direct conection with students and their homeworks.

They can also automatize grading process, allowing students stay active in view of their progress and course grades.
