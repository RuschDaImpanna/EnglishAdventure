Data Treatment Policies

English Adventure! Learning Platform


Created by Viviana Andrea Castañeda in the context of her doctoral research.
Exclusively use for the Institución Educativa Distrital Rincón Santo
Last Update: July 2025


This policy establish the guidelines for personal data treatment compiled by the educative platform English Adventure!, developed by Santiago Andrés Sánchez with exclusively academics intentions, within the framework of the doctoral research of Viviana Andrea Castañeda, and intended for exclusive use for the educational community of the Institución Educativa Distrital Rincón Santo.


The personal data treatment is executed in pursuance of the Law 1581 of 2012, the Decree 1377 of 2013 and others normative provisions valid in Colombia in subject of personal data treatment.



1. Treatment responsable


The responsable for personal data treatment compiled through the English Adventure! platform is its developer, Santiago Andrés Sánchez, who manage and protect the information in accomplishment to the legal duty and techniques that guarantee its security, trustworthiness and confidentiality.



2. Compiled data

By entering to the English Adventure! platform by institutional authentication mechanisms (as Google Auth), the following personal data is collected and stored:

  • Full name
  • E-mail address verified by the system
  • Unique ID of the account provided by Google
  • Profile name linked to the institucional account

These data is obtained directly from the institutional account of the user, with explicit prior authorization, and are related with the login process and the educative customization use inside the platform.



3. Treatment objectives


The personal information collection will be used only by academical intentions, within the authorized use of the platform by the educational community of the IED Rincón Santo context. The specifical objectives of the treatment include:

  • Allow the individual and secure access to the platform.
  • Asign correclty to each user in their corresponding class or groups.
  • Provide access to content, material and relevant educational resources.
  • Compile usage and participation data that may be analized under the reaserch framework of Viviana Andrea Castañeda, by strictly pedagogical and academics purposes.

Additionally, in cases where user sends comments, suggestions, complaints or request throughout feedback forms, your name, e-mail address and message may be treated jointly with the following purposes:

  • Improve the performance, design or content of the platform.
  • Apply pedagogical or technical adjustments derived by real use from the users.
  • Request technical or academical advice to optimize the educative enviroment.
  • Incorporate, in an anonymous manner if needed, elements of the feedback in the analysis process and platform use as part of the doctoral research.

Research note:


Personal data treatment under the doctoral research context adheres to ethical and legal principles of privacy, and this does not involve the public disclosure of information that could directly identify users on a direct way. Any spread of the results will be done with academical intentions and in compliance with the current regulations about data protection and research ethics.



4. Data holder rights

Personal data holder has the right to:

  • Get to know, update and rectify their data.
  • Request the elimination of their personal data.
  • Revoke treatment authorization, when they deems it appropriate.

To exercise any of these rights, the user must contact the data controller through the means of communication indicated at the end of this policy.



5. Data conservation and elimination

Personal data will be kept only during the time the user is active on the platform. If the data is not being used actively or the user no longer belongs to the educative community of the IED Rincón Santo, will be deleted within a maximum of 30 calendar days, unless there is a technical or legal reason for its temporary conservation.



6. Privacy and security notice

The data controller will execute the technical and administrative measures needed to guarantee the personal data protection. By no means will be shared data with third-partys without prior authorization of the holder, unless by legal duties accomplishment.



7. Attention channels

For queries, claims or request related with this policy, data holders can contact with:

  • Feedback Form available on the platform.
  • Santiago Andrés Sánchez, responsable of data treatment.

English Adventure! use implies the express acceptance of this personal data treatment policy and the informed consent by the user.
